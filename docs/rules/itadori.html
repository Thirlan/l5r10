Itadori Changes

Below are some of the fundamental changes to the original game mechanics under the Itadori ruleset.

Core Goals

  • Simplify rules: combining skills, simplifying weapons, reorganize presentation of material, reduce math complexity, removal of confusing terms
  • Standardize rules: unify trait, skill and other mechanic usage throughout so it is intuitive what to use when.
  • Fix the Political system: Combat is well fleshed out, but the political mechanics and courtier schools are underwhelming. Many Courtier School Ranks appear to be mundane abilities that can be done without the School ability, e.g. Doji Courtier Rank 3: The Perfect Gift asks you to roll "Courtier / Awareness at TN 20 to come up with a suitable gift".
  • Fix the dueling system: Duels were easy to min-max, resulting in one sided events with little risk. In addition there was no real decision making in the process with just a series of rolls at each step.
  • Errata incorporation: integrating official corrections and updates from the original game materials.

# Short Description Problem Resolution
1 Void and unskilled rolls Skills are less useful than traits because they apply to narrower situations and provide a smaller die bonus. Void points cannot enhance unskilled rolls, improving the value of skills and simplifying the rules.
2 Traits and skills capped at 5 Ranks up to 10 and situational bonuses led to large dice pools and slower play. Cap traits and skills at 5. Supernatural beings, monsters, and Kami may have higher caps.
3 Skill mastery Many mastery bonuses duplicated rank-up bonuses and added complexity without meaningful impact. Remove redundant masteries. Keep and streamline some masteries for all skills; rank 5 skills give +5 Insight.
4 +# TR / -# TR There was no quick term for a bonus or penalty applied to the roll instead of the TN. Use TR (To Your Roll): +5 TR adds 5 to the roll, and -5 TR subtracts 5.
5 Raises and Free Raises Raises and Free Raises were unclear on contested and non-contested rolls. A Raise means -5 TR to your roll. Remove Free Raises and replace them with +5 TR.
6 Emphasis Skill emphases added complexity, and re-rolling 1s was too weak for narrow applications. Remove emphasis as a game term. Keep re-rolls for entire skills and give them to some underpowered schools.
7 Tiles, feet, and meters Feet-only measurements required extra conversion for grid play and players unfamiliar with Imperial units. Show measurements as #t / #' / #m. One tile is always 5' x 5' / 1.5m x 1.5m; for example, 1t / 5' / 1.5m.
8 Bunpitsu (Calligraphy and Forgery) Many small skills were hard to manage. Calligraphy was underused, and Forgery overlapped with it. Combine them into Bunpitsu (文筆, scribecraft), using Intelligence. Include Cipher and Forgery emphases.
9 Political skills and resources Political skill pairings and the resources used by political actions needed clarification. Define core and supporting political skills, clarify trait and resource pairings, split Investigation by approach, and add examples and opposed rolls. Bunpitsu supports correspondence, forgery, and written ciphers.
10 Han-Kyu, Yumi, and Dai-Kyu The bow names were unclear: Yumi is the general term, while Han-Kyu and Dai-Kyu are bow types. Remove Yumi as a weapon entry. Simplify Dai-Kyu so it can be used on foot or horseback.
11 Honor gains and losses The tables were inefficient, inconsistent, incomplete, and unclear about how they mapped to Bushido. Use minor, major, and exceptional/blasphemous categories. Map examples to Bushido principles to support consistent rulings.
12 Redundant conditional effects Some conditions gave the same penalties for very similar situations. Combine redundant effects, such as Fatigued and Fasting.
13 Armor Target Number (ATN) Armor and armor bonus were confusing and inconsistently used for total armor and armor-granted bonuses. Use ATN to refer to the Armor Target Number and distinguish it from an armor bonus.
14 Small blades and grapples Small blades were weak, while grapples were too strong. Allow small blades to be used in grapples.
15 Ranges for ranged and thrown weapons Weapon ranges and range penalties were scattered and inconsistent. Add a standardized table for ranges and penalties.
16 Weapon sizes Weapon size rules and their bonuses or penalties were unclear. Add a standardized table for how weapon sizes affect situations.
17 Deaf Rules and disadvantages for deaf characters were missing, despite references in spells. Create rules for deaf characters.
18 Lore: Medicine Lore is among the least-used categories, so general bonuses to it have little value. Add Medicine to the Lore category. Further details are not specified.
19 Foreign culture Lore Lore is underused, and foreign culture Lore skills are even less attractive. Give foreign culture Lore its own section, powers, and rules. Use that skill for social interaction with other cultures instead of Rokugani political skills.
20 Maximum movement The rule was easy to forget, applied infrequently, and added little challenge or strategy. Remove the maximum movement rule to simplify movement and reduce bookkeeping. This also removes the following spell: Speed of the Waterfall.
21 Lost Love becomes Melancholy Not specified. Rename Lost Love to Melancholy.
22 Phobia Not specified. Causes fear.
23 Luck replaced with Fortunes Not specified. Remove Luck and replace it with Fortunes.
24 Wrath of the Kami Not specified. Make the spell anti-resistance magic.
25 Blind disadvantages Not specified. Standardize them with combat status.
26 Weakness Not specified. Generalize it to cover other disadvantages, split into Physical, and Mental.
27 Water Ring movement penalty The text to explain that a character's movement was reduced, (e.g. "Your Water Ring is reduced by 2 for purposes of determing your movement") was convoluted, because you had to understand it doesn't affect spell casting, and resulted in a two step process to determine how it affected your movement. Introduce the concept of Movement Rating and just say your suffer a -1 MR, which is simplier and doesn't involve discussion of rings.
28 Variable spell casting time Spell changes Add variable casting time to each spell.
29 Shrine protection Spell changes Shrines and portable shrines provide protection against magic.
30 Spell casting does not clearly indicate the basic effects if spell casting Although mentioned everywhere in the flavor text, it is never clearly mentioned that shugenja cast spells with verbal (spoken words/chanting), somatic (hand gestures) and magical visual effects, such as glowing eyes. Clarify these effects in the rule
30 Rank 6 spells Rank 6 spells are extremely powerful and need to be contained somewhat Each spell in Rank 6 can only be cast once per day. Casting creates sounds and lights audible and visible from a mile away.
