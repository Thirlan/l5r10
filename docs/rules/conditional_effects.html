Conditional Effects

Conditional Effects are temporary states that modify a character's rolls, attributes, skills and Armor TN. They can be caused by environmental factors, combat conditions, or magical effects.

Effect Description Mechanical Penalty
Blind The character cannot see their target (darkness, a hood, sand thrown in the eyes).
  • -15 TR to ranged attack
  • -5 TR to melee attack
  • For ATN, instead of adding (5 x Reflexes) + 5, you add Reflexes + 5
  • -2 to your Move Rating
  • Any attempt at a Move Action requires an Athletics / Agility Roll (TN 20) or you are knocked Prone
  • You may not make Perception rolls unless the GM judges that you can use senses other than sight for the roll
Cover A character can benefit from cover only when an attack or other effect originates on the opposite side of something that blocks at least half their body.
  • -3 TR to -5 TR to incoming attack rolls, according to GM discretion
Certain spells that function like projectiles may suffer the Cover penalty to the Spellcasting Roll, according to GM discretion.
Dazed A sharp blow or shock has rattled the character. -5 TR to all rolls until the start of their next Turn.
Deafened The character cannot hear (due to loud noise, magical effect, or being naturally deaf).
  • -5 TR to all rolls involving hearing
  • Cannot make Perception rolls that rely on hearing
  • -5 TR to social skill rolls
  • -5 TR to spell casting rolls
Entangled Bound by rope, net, vines, or similar.
  • Cannot take move actions
  • -10 TR to all physical rolls until freed.
Fatigued Exhausted from sustained effort, lack of sleep, food or water.
  • -5 TR to all rolls
  • Recovers with a full night's rest, or food and water.
Flanking A character gains this benefit when they have an active ally on the opposite side of an enemy and within melee range. +5 TR to the total of melee Attack Rolls against the flanked enemy.
Grappled Locked in a grapple with at least one opponent (see Grappling).
  • Many not take move actions
  • May only take:
    • Grappling actions
    • Free Actions
  • -10 grapple penalty to ATN
Mounted / Higher The character is on a horse or otherwise at a meaningful height advantage over their target. Attackers below the character suffer -3 TR to attack them in melee.
Prone The character is on the ground.
  • -5 TR to attack rolls made by the prone character
  • +5 TR to melee attacks against them
  • -5 TR to ranged attacks against them
  • Standing up is a Simple Action
Poor Visibility A character trying to act in near darkness, fog, smoke, and similar situations where visibility is poor.
  • -5 TR to all ranged attack rolls
  • -5 TR to all Perception-based rolls
  • Certain spells that function like projectiles may suffer the Poor Visibility penalty to the Spellcasting Roll, according to GM discretion.
Stunned Completely incapacitated for a brief moment by a particularly nasty effect.
  • Cannot take any Actions until the start of their next Turn
  • -10 to Armor TN
Flight The character can fly.
  • Ignores difficult terrain if they can fly over it.
Immobilized The character is unable to move.
  • The character cannot take any Move Actions.
  • The character cannot take any actions that would involve their feet.
  • -10 to ATN
