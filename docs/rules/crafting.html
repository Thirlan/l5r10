Crafting

Traps

Use the Craft: Trap skill to make a trap.

Building Traps

You can build a trap in any location that makes sense, unless the trap description prevents it. For example, you can build a trap in a house or in the wilderness.

Some locations make a trap more difficult to build and conceal. The GM can increase the material cost and the craft time for these locations.

Triggering Traps

A trap triggers in one of two ways:

  • Automatic: the trap triggers when a person goes into its area.
  • Manual: a person triggers the trap from a maximum of 10 ft away.

When a person goes into the trap area, that person makes an Investigate (Notice) roll. The base TN is 15. The crafter can increase this TN with Raises.

If the roll succeeds:

  • An automatic trap does not trigger immediately.
  • For a manual trap, the person who controls the trap is revealed, even if they are hidden or around a corner. The rope shows their position.
  • The person can move 5 ft in any direction if they have a Move Action. If not, they stay in the trap area.

If the roll fails:

  • The person who controls a manual trap can trigger it.
  • An automatic trap triggers immediately.
  • The damage and effects of the trap hit automatically. Armor still reduces the damage.

When a group goes into a trap area outside of combat rounds:

  • Each person in the group rolls, in the order that the group selects.
  • The rolls stop when one person succeeds, or when the number of people is more than the trap capacity.
  • The trap capacity is 1 person for each 5x5 ft square. For example, a 10x10 ft trap holds 4 people.
  • All people whose roll failed are caught in the trap.

Detection Trap

Description A simple trip wire. It makes a sound, or it silently shows that a person went through it.
Skill Craft: Trap, TN 10
Craft Time 10 minutes to make and set up at one location on the battlemap
Material Cost & Requirements 1 bu of small material
Trap Dimensions 5x1 ft
Effect One of these effects:
  • A bell rings. People can hear it from a maximum of 100 ft away.
  • A quiet sign shows that a person triggered the trap. You can examine the trap later.
Trigger Automatic
Raises
  • 1 Raise: decrease the craft time by 5 minutes (minimum 5 minutes).
  • 1 Raise: increase the Investigate (Notice) TN by 5.
  • 1 Raise: increase the length by 5 ft (10x1, 15x1, and so on).

Explosives

Top secret. Use the Craft: Explosives skill to make explosives.

  • If gaijin pepper in any form goes to a person who is not a Harrier, only seppuku can atone for the loss.
  • Harriers must get back or destroy lost explosives, at any cost.
  • Some recipes have usage restrictions. A superior must approve their use.

Poisons

Honorable samurai do not use poison. Criminals, assassins, and ninja use it to disable or kill a target without risk to themselves. Generals, duelists, and important courtiers are frequent targets.

Use the Craft: Poison skill to make a poison. Each poison has a delivery method:

  • Ingestion: the target must eat or drink the poison.
  • Wound: the poison must go into the bloodstream, usually on a weapon.
  • Contact: the poison must touch the skin. These are the most dangerous poisons.

Medicine (Antidotes) can reduce or cure the effects of some poisons. Only magic can cure other poisons.

Each poison below has two TNs:

  • Craft: the TN to make the poison with Craft: Poison. This assumes that many herbal components are available.
  • Heal: the TN to reduce the symptoms with Medicine (Antidotes).

The GM can use these poisons as a guide to make new poisons.

Dripping Poison

Assassins prefer this poison. Its thick consistency lets it move slowly down a thread onto the lips of a sleeping target. The only delivery method is ingestion. Its strong flavor makes it easy to notice in food. The effects start four hours after ingestion. The target feels sharp stomach pain and strong nausea. Many people think it is food poisoning or illness.

Effects:

  • The target makes a Stamina roll (TN 20). If the roll fails, the target's Stamina decreases by 2.
  • The target is Fatigued, whether the roll succeeds or fails.
  • The target makes this roll again each hour, until the poison is treated or 24 hours pass. After 24 hours, all symptoms stop.
  • If Stamina decreases to 0, Earth and Wounds also become 0 and the target dies.
  • Craft: TN 20
  • Heal: TN 15

Fire Biter

This poison is very painful. It disables more than other poisons, but it kills less often. It must go into the bloodstream. It is too bitter to put in food without detection. Usually, a brush puts it on a weapon, and a wound from that weapon delivers it. It causes painful swelling of the joints, worst near the wound. Some targets also get violent spasms.

Effects:

  • Within ten minutes, the target is Stunned for six hours.
  • If the target succeeds on a Stamina roll (TN 30), the target is Fatigued instead of Stunned.
  • Craft: TN 25
  • Heal: TN 25 (changes the effect from Stunned to Fatigued)

Night Milk

The Shosuro family made this poison first. Other groups also use it, possibly with Scorpion Clan help, to send attention away from the Scorpion. It is a thick, white liquid that must go into the bloodstream. Ingestion does not work. The symptoms start almost immediately. They are short but very strong. Usually, it is painted on weapons. Be careful, because it leaves a white residue that is easy to see.

Effects:

  • The target makes a Stamina roll (TN 25) immediately, and again each minute.
  • If a roll fails, the target is Dazed until the next roll.
  • The effect stops after ten minutes.
  • Craft: TN 25
  • Heal: TN 20 (stops the effects)

Venom

The Shosuro family are the best poison masters in Rokugan. They teach that natural poisons, such as spider or snake venom, can replace complex mixtures. Venom is difficult to collect. But it is less suspicious, and many people think its signs are natural. Sometimes an assassin puts the venomous animal near the target instead of applying the venom.

Effects:

  • When venom goes into the bloodstream, the target's Agility, Reflexes, Stamina, and Strength decrease by 1 each hour.
  • At the start of the second hour and each hour after, the target can make a Stamina roll (TN 25). If the roll succeeds, the target gets no new penalties for that hour.
  • The penalties continue until one of these occurs:
    • The target succeeds on three rolls in sequence.
    • The poison is treated.
    • 24 hours pass.
    • The target rests for eight hours without interruption.
  • If Stamina decreases to 0, Earth and Wounds also become 0 and the target dies.
  • If the target lives, the Traits return to normal after 24 hours.
  • Craft: TN 10 (to prepare the venom for use)
  • Heal: TN 15 (stops new penalties)
