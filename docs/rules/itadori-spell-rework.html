Itadori Spell Rework

This section details the rework of spells in the Itadori system, including changes, removals, and additions to improve gameplay balance and clarity.

Objectives

  • Ensure each spell category has a clear role and purpose.
  • Remove redundant or weak spells
  • Balance the power level of spells
  • Avoid the Tippyverse problem with spells that could break the setting's societal or environmental rules.

Spell Categories and purposes

Spell Changes

General List of reasons for change

Reason Description
YAGNI You Aint Gonna Need It: unlikely to be useful given the limited spell casting choices and other spells you can pick from.
Tippyverse Breaks the way the society works in the game. Example, if teleports are ubiquitous, it would undermine the setting's travel and communication limitations.
Overlap Significant overlap with other spells or Elements, making other spells or elements redundant or less useful.
Simplify Unnecessarily complicated and can be simplified for clarity and ease of use.
Nerf Too powerful for its level and needs to be adjusted in some way.
