Terminology

A full list of all terminology in the game

Term Definition
DR Damage Rating. The dice that an attack or a spell rolls and keeps for damage, written as XkY. Example: a katana has a DR of 3k2.
Equipped The character carries the weapon. The weapon does not need to be drawn or unsheathed. An effect that requires "Equipped" applies only while the character carries the weapon.
Weapon Drawn The character holds the weapon drawn and ready to use. An effect that requires "Weapon Drawn" applies only while the weapon is drawn.
