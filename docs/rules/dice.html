Dice in Legend of the Five Rings

10 sided dice

Legend of the Five Rings exclusively uses ten-sided dice (d10) for resolving actions.

Roll and Keep

In Legend of the Five Rings, Roll and keeping dice, denoted as N k M indicates that you roll N dice and then only keep M dice of your choice. So 3k1 means you roll 3 D10s and then keep only 1.

You may keep any of the dice of your choice, which can result in interesting scenarios where you may want to purposefully fail a roll, if say for example you do not wish to deal damage that would kill a person, or in more nefarious moments you may choose to not heal that pompous bureaucrat as much as you could have.

Roll and Keep is also sometimes abbreviated as Skill / Trait, which indicates (Skill + Trait) k Trait. So if you have Kenjutsu 2 and Agility 3, then you'd have 5k3.

Exploding Dice

In Legend of the Five Rings, exploding means that if a certain number on a die is rolled, normally a 10, then you can keep the 10 and then roll an additional die that is added on top of a that die.

The die you rolled can itself explode, leading to potentially very high results if you keep rolling 10s. This adds a level of lethality to Legend of the Five Rings as there will come a point where someone will at some point roll an unexpected high number on you. Life is fleeting use it wisely...

Re-rolling a die

Some powers will allow you to re-roll a die or entire dice rolls.
