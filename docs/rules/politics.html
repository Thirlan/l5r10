Politics in Rokugan

Politics in the Emerald Empire is the art of navigating the relationships, obligations, hierarchies, and invisible pressures that bind Rokugan together. A political character understands who has authority, who has influence, what behavior is expected, and how to apply pressure without destroying their own position.

Political actions are generally built around five core skills: Courtier, Etiquette, Intimidation, Sincerity, and Temptation. Investigation also plays an important supporting role. Their effectiveness is shaped by Honor, Status, Fame, Favors, and Money.

Political Resources

Honor

Honor is your moral compass, internal discipline, and the external reputation created by your conduct. It is primarily a defensive resource that supports Willpower when resisting political pressure, particularly through Etiquette and Sincerity.

Common uses:

  • Resist manipulation, intimidation, or temptation.
  • Maintain composure when publicly pressured or humiliated.
  • Support the credibility of your statements.
  • Avoid behavior inappropriate to your station.

Status

Status is your formal authority within the Celestial Order. It represents rank, office, station, and the authority that comes with them. Status is hard power: it gives people a reason to obey you.

Status is added to active uses of Etiquette and Intimidation. It helps you order someone to obey, navigate bureaucracy, access restricted information, invoke rank-based privileges, and establish proper conduct.

A character's Status does not automatically make them persuasive. A high-ranking official may be obeyed while remaining deeply disliked.

Fame

Fame is the political weight created by being known and recognized throughout the Empire. It is soft power: people may listen because of who you are and what your reputation represents.

Fame is circumstantial. It benefits you only when the people you are dealing with recognize and respect the reputation that created it. Fame is commonly added to Courtier and Perform: Oratory rolls, but not to a hostile action against someone or someone who does not respect your reputation.

Money

Money can purchase goods and services or be used for bribery. Turning money directly into political influence is generally dishonorable and may result in Honor loss. Unlike Status or Fame, money does not automatically grant political legitimacy.

Political Traits

Awareness

Awareness is the ability to read people, situations, and the invisible currents of a political environment. It tells you who is important, who is uncomfortable, who wants something, when a conversation is shifting, and where the pressure points in a negotiation lie.

Example: During a Winter Court negotiation, a Crane courtier notices that the Lion ambassador becomes tense whenever military supplies are mentioned. Awareness reveals the hidden pressure points before the courtier decides how to exploit them.

Willpower

Willpower is the ability to remain yourself when someone else is trying to make you crack. It helps you keep your voice steady, expression controlled, and behavior appropriate when questioned, insulted, intimidated, or tempted.

Example: A rival publicly accuses a samurai of betraying his lord. Willpower allows the samurai to remain composed rather than taking the bait and appearing guilty.

Political Skills

Etiquette

Etiquette is knowing the rules of the social and bureaucratic game and using them without causing offense. It covers proper procedures, rituals, hierarchy, customs, formal greetings, social expectations, and bureaucracy.

Sincerity

Sincerity is the ability to be believed. It does not determine whether you are telling the truth; it determines whether others believe what you say. A truthful character can fail by panicking or choosing words poorly, while a liar can succeed by remaining composed.

Courtier

Courtier uses social influence, reputation, conversation, and political maneuvering to shape the behavior of others. It is the primary expression of soft power

Intimidation

Intimidation uses hard power to make someone understand the consequences of refusing you. It is paired with Awareness + Status because effective intimidation requires both understanding of the target and legitimate authority behind the threat.

Temptation

Temptation offers someone something they want badly enough to overcome their normal restraint. Common uses include bribery, seduction, material rewards, and exploiting personal desires.

Commerce

Commerce handles the economic side of politics and trade. It is normally paired with Intelligence and is considered a relatively low-status skill in Rokugan.

Investigation

Investigation is divided between observing the physical world and observing people. Perception investigates physical evidence; Awareness investigates social circumstances. Investigation is not itself a political skill and does not receive Status or Fame.

Gifts and Favors

Gift giving is a political ritual that introduces the giver, declares a relationship, and reinforces Status. It is especially important when visiting someone of equal or higher Status, attending a wedding, or attending Winter Court as a Courtier.

Giving Ritual

The giver rolls Etiquette / Awareness + Status vs. TN 10 x the recipient's Status. The recipient rolls Etiquette / Willpower + Honor vs. TN 15. The recipient receives +5 TR when receiving a Perfect Gift. Failure by the recipient is a Minor Breach of Etiquette. The ritual succeeds only if both characters succeed; on success, the giver gains +1 Fame.

Bribery

Bribery converts economic power into political influence through dishonorable means. The bribe begins at the target's Status x 10 koku, with a minimum of 1 koku. The briber suffers Honor loss for a Minor Crime, then rolls Temptation / Awareness against Etiquette / Willpower + Honor.

RequestTarget bonus
Minor: look the other way or grant a Favor+0
Medium: spare a family from torture+5
Major: dismiss a legal case+10
Extreme: betray friends and family+15

Offering +10 koku gives the briber +1 to the roll. Offering +30 koku increases the Favors gained by +1. If the briber fails, the situation normally reverses and risks exposing the briber.

Breaches of Etiquette

Minor breaches include losing emotional control, improper introductions, speaking too casually to someone of higher Status, discussing money openly, dressing incorrectly, failing to offer a small gift, and asking inappropriate personal questions.

Major breaches include touching someone unnecessarily, violating Status boundaries, entering restricted spaces, insulting a superior, riding through high-ranking districts, or failing to bow when passing someone of higher rank.

Blasphemous breaches include insulting the Imperial Family or a Great Clan Daimyo, desecrating sacred sites, disrespecting religious objects, violating funerary taboos, breaking a sworn oath, or disrupting a tea ceremony.
