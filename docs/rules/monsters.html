Monsters

Game play

This section details the rules for monsters. It is important to note that a monster counts as a fully playable character that follows the rules of the game, including taking actions, rolling dice, using skills, and being subject to damage and other effects.

Experience points

Monsters do not generally gain experience points and as such cannot purchase new skills or increase traits beyond the ones they have. GMs may of course relax this rule at their discretion.

Wound Ranks

Monsters follow a simpler wound rank system than characters for the purpose of minimizing book keeping for the GM.

  • There are only 4 ranks: Healthy, Hurt, Crippled and Dead
  • Healthy, Hurt and Crippled are all 5x their Earth Ring
Level Penalty Description
Healthy — The Monster is unharmed or only superficially marked.
Hurt -10 to all rolls Significant injuries; the Monster is starting to stumble.
Crippled -20 to all rolls The Monster can barely fight on.
Dead — The Monster is no longer able to act and is effectively dead.

Example

A wolf has Earth 2 which would give it the following wound rank table:

Level Penalty Amount
Healthy — 10 and below
Hurt -10 11 to 20
Crippled -20 21 to 30
Dead — 31+
