Combat

The rules below are based on the 4th edition of Legend of the Five Rings, with a number of house rules drawn from the Itadori revisions (movement simplification, removal of Full Defense and Center stances, conflation of Fasting into Fatigued, and the conditional effect adjustments).

Definition of Terms

  • Action: Anything a character does that takes some amount of focused effort. Actions come in three sizes — Free, Simple, and Complex — and most things that happen in combat are resolved through one or more Actions.
  • Initiative: The score that determines the order in which characters act during combat.
  • Round: A single pass through the Initiative order. Every participant gets one Turn per Round. A Round represents roughly six seconds of in-fiction time.
  • Turn: A character's portion of a Round. On a character's Turn they may take one Complex Action or two Simple Actions, plus any number of Free Actions (within reason), and they may move as described under Move Action.

Sequence of Events

Declare stances

At the start of the first round of combat, all characters declare their initial stances simultaneously, before any initiative is rolled. If no stance is declared it is assumed to be the attack stance. In the subsequent rounds, characters may change their stance on their turn.

Initiative

At the start of combat each participant rolls Initiative Insight Rank / Reflexes. Ties are broken by the higher Reflexes Trait, then Insight Rank, then by Void Ring, then by a contested roll. The resulting order is used for every Round of the encounter unless an effect modifies it. Characters begin combat in Attack Stance by default.

Mounted Initiative: When a character is mounted, both the rider and the mount roll Initiative and the lower of the two results is used for both, so that rider and mount act on the same count.

Turns

Beginning with the highest Initiative and proceeding down, each character takes their Turn. On their Turn a character chooses or changes a Stance (a Free Action), spends their Actions, and resolves any movement. When every character has acted the Round ends, durations tick down, and a new Round begins at the top of the Initiative order.

Reactions

At the end of a Round, when all characters have taken their Turn, there are certain abilities that take effect as a result of the events of the Round. For example, spell effects that have expired end during the Reaction Stage. All such effects happen simultaneously during Stage 3. Reaction effects, typically generated by Techniques, Spells, or Advantages are always clearly labeled as such.

Attack and Defense

Attack Roll

An attack is a Skill Roll using the appropriate weapon Skill (Kenjutsu for swords, Kyujutsu for bows, Polearms for spears and naginata, Jiujutsu for unarmed, and so on) combined with a Trait — usually Agility for melee and ranged weapons, or Reflexes for thrown weapons and a few special techniques. The roll is Skill/Trait, attempting to meet or exceed the target's Armor TN.

If the attack succeeds, the attacker rolls the weapon's damage dice plus the rolled dice for their Strength Trait (or other Trait specified by the weapon) and the defender must make a Wound Check.

Defense (Armor TN)

A character's Armor TN is the target number an attacker must meet to hit them. It is built from:

  • Base: 5 + (Reflexes × 5).
  • Armor: Add the Armor TN bonus of any worn armor (Ashigaru +3, Light +5, Heavy +10, etc.).
  • Stance: Defense Stance adds Air Ring + Defense Skill. Full Attack Stance reduces Armor TN by 10.
  • Cover, position, and conditions: See the Conditional Effects table for situational modifiers.

Wounds

Each character has a number of Wound Levels, each with a threshold equal to Earth Ring × 2 (the first level, Healthy, × 5). When a character takes damage, compare their accumulated Wounds to the levels below. The wound level a character currently occupies imposes the listed penalty on all rolls.

Damage Reduction

Armor also provides a flat Damage Reduction that is subtracted from the total damage of every attack that hits.

Healing Wounds

  • Natural Rest: A character at rest heals an amount of Wounds equal to their Stamina × their Insight Rank each day. A full day of bed rest doubles this.
  • Medicine: An ally with the Medicine Skill may roll Medicine / Intelligence (TN 15) once per day per patient. On a success the patient heals additional Wounds equal to the roll's raises plus the healer's School Rank. The Medicine (Wound Treatment) Emphasis is the standard application.
  • Out Characters: A character at the Out level requires a successful Medicine roll (TN 20) within minutes or risks death; otherwise they remain unconscious until brought back above Out by healing.

Stances

A character begins combat in Attack Stance and may change Stance once per Turn as a Free Action at the start of their Turn.

Attack Stance

The default, balanced posture. No bonuses and no penalties. A character in Attack Stance acts normally.

Full Attack Stance

The character commits fully to the offensive. They gain +1k1 to all attack rolls and add their Fire Ring to the total of each attack roll. In exchange, their Armor TN is reduced by 10. Full Attack Stance is usable while mounted.

Defense Stance

The character focuses on protecting themselves. They add (Air Ring + Defense Skill) to their Armor TN, but may take only Free Actions and a single Simple Action that turn. Spells cannot be cast while in Defense Stance.

Defense Zoning: While in Defense Stance, any other character that wishes to move into a square within the reach of the defender's weapon (1 square for a katana, 2 for spears and other reach weapons) must spend an additional 1 square / 5 feet of movement to enter that square.

Maneuvers

Maneuvers are special options layered on top of an attack. Unless otherwise noted a Maneuver is taken as part of an attack roll and is declared before dice are rolled. Most Maneuvers either modify the attack with a number of Raises or replace the attack's normal effect.

Called Shot (Variable Difficulty)

Add Raises to your attack roll to target a specific location (an arm, a leg, a weapon, an item being carried). Effects are determined by the GM — a successful arm shot might force a Disarm contest, a leg shot might trigger Knockdown, and so on. Typical cost: -10 to the roll.

Disarm (-10 TR)

This maneuver does normal damage. If the attack hits, the defender must make a contested Strength or weapon-Skill / Reflexes roll against the attacker's roll or drop their weapon into an adjacent square. Kicking a weapon along the ground is a Free Action and sends it up to 10 feet away.

Push (-5 TR)

You shove your enemy backward, possibly into deadly circumstances. If successful, the attack deals normal damage and forces a Contested Strength Roll between the character and the target. If you are successful, the target stumbles 10 feet backward.

Extra Attacks (-25 TR)

An attacker may take an additional attack with a penalty on the original attack. School Techniques and abilities that grant "attack twice" replace this and allow a second Simple-Action attack on the same Turn without the Raise cost.

Increase Damage (Variable TR)

For each -5 to the attack roll, add +1k0 to the damage roll if the attack hits.

Knockdown (-10 TR or -20 TR)

If the attack hits, damage is dealt normally, and the defender must succeed at a contested Strength roll with the attacker, or be knocked Prone. Attempting to knockdown a two legged creature is -10 and a four legged creature is -20.

Grappling

Grappling is a unique combat situation that changes the flow of normal combat for those involved. A character may initiate a grapple as covered in the table below, and once in a grapple the characters involved must make a contested Jiujutsu / Strength roll at the beginning of their turn to determine if they can act on their turn. If they fail then they may do nothing until their next turn other than a Free Action.

If a character has a small blade in hand during the grapple, they may choose to roll Small Blades / Agility instead of Jiujutsu / Strength for the purposes of grappling. If they do so though, they can only choose the weapon or break action.

Conditional Effects

Conditional Effects are temporary states that modify a character's rolls and Armor TN. Where a 4th edition effect originally used a dice-pool penalty, the Itadori revisions use a flat TN penalty instead.

Blind The character cannot see their target (darkness, a hood, sand thrown in the eyes).
  • -15 TR to ranged attack
  • -5 TR to melee attack

  • -3 TR to -5 TR to incoming attack rolls, according to GM discretion

  • Cannot take move actions

  • -5 TR to all rolls
  • Recovers with a full night's rest, or food and water.
