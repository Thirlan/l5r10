Disadvantages

Character Creation

At character creation, you may only gain at most 10 points from disadvantages. You may gain additional disadvantages, but they will give you no additional points.

After Character Creation

Characters may gain some of these disadvantages after character creation due to events in their campaigns. They will be given WITHOUT the bonus experience points, but for purposes of certain abilities you will still count their point costs.

A word of caution

Disadvantages are not to be taken lightly. Many of the disadvantages will provide far less points than the penalties they introduce and this is intentional even in the original 4th edition rules. Most 1 to 2 point disadvantages are minor inconveniences, with 3 to 4s being punitive under certain circumstances, lastly with 5 and above being extreme with failure often leading to death.

Social

Socially Inept

Points 2/4
+1 bonus exp Crab
Flavor You are socially inept in some way that makes it difficult for you to interact with others.
Rules
  • 2 points: -3 TR to all skills with the Social keyword
  • 4 points: -5 TR to all skills with the Social keyword

Below are examples of social ineptness:

NameDescription
Anti-social You find the presence of others uncomfortable, so much so that it is immediately obvious to those around you. Your preference for solitude causes you to behave in a manner that might best be described as rude.
Selfish You must get your way, and others must give way to your wishes. You have no respect for the opinions or desires of others.

Social Complications

Points 3/6
+1 bonus exp
Flavor Your social life is entangled with a person, relationship, obligation, or secret that continually creates problems for you. This may be someone who loves or hates you, a family that has rejected you, an unwanted marriage or dependent, a dangerous secret, or an enemy working against you from the shadows. Whatever form it takes, this complication regularly interferes with your duties, relationships, reputation, or freedom of action.
Rules

A warning to players. This disadvantage requires working with your GM to flesh out the nature of the problem. Be aware that this disadvantage will result in someone regularly causing problems to you from a position of advantage throughout the campaign, and as such you must be prepared to accept the consequences for this.

Consider buying this advantage multiple times if you wish to simulate increasing degrees of severity for the complication. Example, obligation with 3 points could imply a regular obligation such as siding with the Phoenix clan during negotiations even though your Lord request you to side with the Crane. An obligation with 6 points could imply having to steal some artifact from your clan.

Below are examples of social complications you might consider:

Name Description
Secret Love Someone is madly in love with you and will go to any lengths to sabotage your romantic interests, even politically arranged marriages, in order to ensure that you remain available for them.
Unknown Enemy Someone somewhere in another Clan hates you, and wishes to see you dead. You have no idea who they are or why they hate you, but you will very soon.
Bitter Bethrothal You have been promised to another, or perhaps are already married, and neither of you are at all pleased with the situation. Your spouse despises you, but he or she at least has the decorum to manage your estate properly... for the most part. Your mutual dislike, however, can result in tremendous difficulty with even simple domestic or bureaucratic tasks.
Blackmailed Someone out there knows of some dark secret of yours, or some horrible indiscretion you committed in a moment of weakness. What's worse, they intend to make you pay for it. The cost of this Disadvantage is equal to your Status Rank. The person blackmailing you will contact you periodically and require certain things of you. You must weigh these actions against the potential disgrace if your secret is discovered, and act accordingly.
Blacksheep For whatever reason, your family is completely disgusted with you and your actions. You have not been cast out and made ronin, but you are no longer welcome in your own household. This may cause serious problems when trying to advance School Rank, going to great lengths to find a willing teacher.
Dark Secret You possess some horrific secret that could lead not only to your ruination, but perhaps others in your family as well. It may be something terrible you have learned about your family that you alone know (if a venerated ancestor was secretly a dishonorable buffoon), or something you personally have done (you may be a member of the Kolat or Gozoku). If your secret is exposed, you may be required to commit seppuku or possibly even be executed.
Dependent Someone who is largely incapable of defending himself or herself requires you to look after them. This may be an elderly relative, a naive spouse, or a small child.
Sworn Enemy You have earned an enemy, one who will stop at nothing to see you defeated or even killed.
Obligation There is someone to whom you are indebted or for whom you must fulfill some significant duty.
Hostage In the resolution of a dispute between Clans, it is not uncommon for hostages to be exchanged as "guests" of one another's Clan's for a specified amount of time. This ensures that the treaty in question is not broken. Although you are treated respectfully and as a guest in the lands of another Clan, you are not permitted to leave without escort and, should war break out between your Clan and your hosts, your life will be forfeit.

Bounty

Points 2/4/6
+1 bonus exp
Flavor A price has been placed on your head due to your involvement (or suspected involvement) in a crime.
Rules The severity of the crime itself dictates the cost of this Disadvantage, the amount of the bounty on your head, and who will be willing to hunt for you. Depending on the level of the offense the reward increases, the TN to recognize you with Lore: Law / Perception decreases and the breadth of the search expands as covered in the table below.
PointsCrimeReward rangeTN to recognize youBreadth of search
2 Minor offense 1-5 Koku 25 Magistrates from that province, or ronin who hunt for bounties
4 Serious offense 10-15 Koku 15 Any magistrate from the Clan which assigned the bounty
6 Violent or heinous act 20+ Koku 10 Magistrates from every Great Clan, and possibly famous Bounty Hunters
  • A minor offense (2 points) - Lore: Law / Perception roll at TN 25 against you: generates a bounty of 1-5 Koku, and generally only magistrates from that province, or ronin who hunt for bounties, will search for you.
  • A serious offense (4 points) - Lore: Law / Perception roll at TN 15 against you: generates a bounty of 10-15 Koku. Any magistrate from the Clan which assigned the bounty will be aware of it.
  • A violent or heinous act (6 points) - Lore: Law / Perception roll at TN 10 against you: generates a bounty of at least 20 Koku. Magistrates from every Great Clan are on the lookout for you, and you are likely to be executed in short order if taken into custody. The offended Clan may even send a specific request to the Tsuruchi Bounty Hunters if your crime was exceptionally awful.

Debt

Points 2/4/6
+1 bonus exp
Flavor Whether through bad luck or simple imprudence, at some point you found yourself short of money and "solved" this problem by taking out a loan, either from a wealthy commoner or a fellow samurai. You are expected to repay this loan, and until you do, the shame of your indebted state hangs over you.
Rules

If others learn about your debt, you will lose Fame and your fellow samurai may come to look down on you; your lord may also disapprove of your irresponsibility. In the long term, a persistently indebted samurai may be publicly shamed, losing Status or worse. Moreover, even if you keep the debt secret, your creditor may decide to use it to pressure you into doing things on his behalf (this should be handled by the GM through role-play). This Disadvantage is worth one additional point for courtiers.

Character Flaws

Points 3
Rules

Your character has notable flaws that impact their behavior and interactions. These flaws are significant enough to influence the story and may be triggered under certain circumstances. When triggered you must make a Willpower + Honor roll against a TN of 20. If you fail, your GM will determine the appropriate consequences.

A warning to players: these flaws may temporarily take control of your characters away from you. This is intended to create drama and story opportunities, not to kill your characters, so be prepared for their consequences.
Name Flavor Rules +1 Bonus Exp
Brash You have a notoriously short temper and are likely to answer anything you perceive as a threat with steel. Whenever you are the target of a threat (e.g. Intimidation roll that isn't an Order) or insult (e.g. Courtier roll with the intent to offend), regardless of the result of the roll, you must make a resist roll. Failure will result in your character taking an appropriately aggressive action, such as an issuing an Iaijitsu duel or a temper tantrum in court. Lion
Contrary Your strong will and rigid view of the world insists not only that you develop an opinion on everything, but that you must share it at every opportunity. During ANY conversation, be it a debate, dispute, or even a casual conversation, you must roll to resist or enter the conversation and take a side, even if it is inconvenient or socially awkward. The conversation becomes unpleasant as you labor on issues, refusing to move on even after having made your point. This will result in a minor/major loss of honor as a failure in Courtesy, or other social consequences as decided by the GM. Imperial, Courtier
Insensitive Your primary interest is in yourself and things that you care about. The plight of others simply does not interest you, and you find it difficult to pretend otherwise. Any time you would perform an action that would benefit others, with the exception of your Lord, you must resist or be unable to act. Scorpion
Jealousy You are obsessed with outperforming another individual in order to prove that you deserve all the things they have, things you perceive as superior to your own. You are obsessed with overcoming them at all costs, and will go to any lengths to do so. Choose one other PC or a major NPC in your campaign. Any time an opportunity arises to directly compete against them, or some indirect task that will increase your Fame or Status above theirs, regardless of the risk associated with it, you must resist or you will pursue it at the cost of family and honor. Once you have bested the target of your jealousy, your nature will cause you to become jealous of someone else.
Melancholy You tend to suffer bouts of melancholy when reminded of something you lost, whether it is a loved one (child, parent, sibling, lover) or a treasured possession.

Common occurrences will trigger your melancholy, for example if you lost a child then: seeing children playing, a family going about their day, or even a simple mention of someone losing something will require you to roll to resist melancholy.

Your rolls don't explode. The effect ends when you either:

  • rest
  • or spend 1 void point, which causes your void to be lower by 1 until you rest.
Lechery Physical pleasure is a weakness of yours, and you spend considerable time pursuing it. When meeting with an attractive individual, or when you have an opportunity to either chase someone or go to the Red Light district, you must roll to resist.
Overconfident You possess a grossly inflated view of your own prowess, and are often incapable of recognizing when a situation is beyond your ability to handle. When given choices that reward you with honor, fame, money, or experience, you will always take the opportunity unless you resist, even if it is risky or unwise. For example, you will accept duels, missions and refuse to retreat, because you believe deep down that you will succeed against the odds. Lion, Mantis
Gossiper You cannot resist the temptation to spread rumors and gossip, no matter how ridiculous or outlandish they might seem. Any time you are presented with any information on an individual, you must roll to resist or make a Courtier roll for the purposes of spreading gossip. Courtier
True Love True love in Rokugan can be a wonderful experience, but more often than not it is a disaster, for it is rare for samurai to be allowed to marry for love. Any time you make a choice that will get between you and your lover, you must roll to resist or follow your heart, which can lead to complications.
Obsession / Compulsion You are consumed by something, which you are hopelessly compelled to partake. This may be a goal you are determined to accomplish, a subject you desperately wish to understand, or an interest you cannot resist pursuing. Family, friends, material possessions, and even Bushido may become secondary to satisfying your Obsession / Compulsion. This may be something illicit and addictive, such as sake or gambling, or studying some forbidden texts. If doing so would require a dishonorable action, you must either accept the consequences or resist the compulsion. Even when pursuing your obsession is dangerous, scandalous, or blasphemous, you find it extremely difficult to turn away. Note: This is the general catch all for those not already covered above.
