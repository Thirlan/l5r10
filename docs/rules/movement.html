Movement & Distances

Distances

In this game, distances are measured in feet as well as squares for purposes of a grid-based tactical map. Each grid is assumed to be 5 feet across. This allows for a level of abstraction away from feet vs meters.

Throughout the text both feet and square will be written out for this purpose in the format of X sq / Y', where X is the number of squares and Y is the equivalent distance in feet. X obviously is Y divided by 5.

Move Action

It is important to note that in this edition, Free action move is no longer different than a Simple move action.

Move Rating

A character's Move Rating is the movement allowance per Move Action. This is calculated as 4 sq / 20' + 1 sq / 5' per Water Ring Rank. See the table below to see what this comes out to for each Water Ring Rank.

Water Ring Squares per Move Action Feet
1 5 25
2 6 30
3 7 35
4 8 40
5 9 45

Throughout the texts you will see reference to sentences such as "-1 to your Move Rating", which will imply your Water Ring is -1 for the purposes of determining your Move Rating.

Terrain Difficulty

Certain terrains will impede movement as shown below.

Terrain Cost Examples
Basic No Penalty Flat ground, open road, dojo floor, light grass.
Moderate -1 sq / 5' for every move through this terrain Mud, shallow water, light undergrowth, gravel slopes, broken paving, snow.
Difficult -2 sq / 10' for every move through this terrain Dense forest, deep snow, knee-deep water, sheer rubble, climbing.

In effect this means to move through a single tile of shallow water requires 2 sq / 10' of movement. A dense forest would be 3 sq / 15' of movement per tile.

Maximum Movement

The total movement of tiles / feet that a character can traverse is limited by (4 + Water Ring) x 3 sq / 15' per Move Action. This is simply previous table's values x 3, or in other words, you will not hit the cap as long as you only have your normal 2 x Simple actions and free action to move.

A few spells (i.e. Speed of the waterfall) and items, can increase someone's maximum movement.

In practice this means most characters with additional movement actions or magically increased movement rates should use at least one or more of their actions for non-movement purposes non-movement actions combined with any additional movement they can perform within their maximum movement for the turn.

It is important to note that this is NOT the distance in tiles / feet. So someone who moved 5 sq / 25' with Water 1 in difficult terrain (-2 sq / 10' per tile) would still count as having hit their max of 15 sq / 70' for the turn. Even if they were given additional movement actions, they would still be limited by their maximum movement for the turn.

Water Ring Squares per Move Action Feet
1 15 70
2 18 90
3 21 105
4 24 120
5 27 135
