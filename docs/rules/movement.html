Movement & Distances

Distances

In this game, distances are measured in feet and meters as well as tiles for a grid-based tactical map. Each tile is assumed to be 5' / 1.5m across. This allows for a level of simplification and abstraction between measurement systems.

Throughout the text both tiles, feet and meters will be written out in the format of Xt / Y' / Zm, where:

  • X is the number of tiles
  • Y is the equivalent distance in feet
  • Z is the equivalent distance in meters

X is just Y/5 since all tiles are 5' / 1.5m across.

Move Action

It is important to note that in this edition, Free action move is no longer different than a Simple move action.

Move Rating

A character's Move Rating (MR) is the movement allowance per Move Action. This is a point system that is spent to move a number of tiles / feet / meters. The reason why it is a point system is because difficult terrain and other modifiers actually dictate how many tiles / feet / meters a character must spend to move through it. The normal ratio for conversion is 1 MR to 1t / 5' / 1.5m.

The number of MRs you get is 4 + your Water Ring Rank. See the table below to see what this comes out to for each Water Ring Rank.

Water Ring Movement Rating (MR) Normal Tile Feet Meters
1 5 5 25 7.6
2 6 6 30 9.1
3 7 7 35 10.7
4 8 8 40 12.2
5 9 9 45 13.7

Throughout the texts you will see reference to sentences such as "-1 to your Move Rating" or just "-1 MR", which will imply you lose 1 MR point.

Terrain Difficulty

In the example before we assumed a basic terrain tile with no penalty, but as show in the table below, if a tile is a difficult terrain then it can cost additional MR to move through it.

Terrain MR Cost Examples
Normal 1 Flat ground, open road, dojo floor, light grass.
Moderate 2 Mud, shallow water, light undergrowth, gravel slopes, broken paving, snow.
Difficult 3 Dense forest, deep snow, knee-deep water, sheer rubble, climbing.

In effect this means to move through a single tile of shallow water requires 2 MR or a dense forest would be 3 MR.

Minimum Movement Action

If a charater has:

  • at least 1 MR
  • not already moved 1t / 5' / 1.5m or more for their turn
  • not Conditional Effect: immobilized.
  • not Conditional Effect: entangled.
  • not Conditional Effect: grappled.

Then they may choose to forfeit all actions for the turn to move 1t / 5' / 1.5m regardless of Terrain Difficulty penalties.
