Money

Currency

Currency in Rokugan is measured in koku, with 1 koku being roughly equivalent to the amount of rice needed to feed one person for a year. Over time however this unit of measurement has diverged due to inflation and regional differences.

As it stands to today the currency is composed of the following:

  • 1 koku
  • 5 bu = 1 koku
  • 10 zeni = 1 bu

Monthly stipends

Every month, a Samurai's faction will provide them with more money. Ronin, monks, and similarly resourceless characters do not receive this benefit. The formula is:

School starting money x Status / 6, rounded down

Example: Doji Haru's Doji Courtier school provides him with 10 starting Koku. He is still a common Samurai with status 1 and as such receives: 1 koku, 3 bu, 3 Zeni

To receive this stipend, a Samurai must present themselves to their designated clan official in a given village, town or city. It is possible for a given location to have no such individual, requiring the samurai to travel to the nearest location where one is available.

Weapon sizes

Weapon sizes affect certain skill rolls:

  • Conceal: Sleight of Hand skill rolls to conceal the weapon on yourself.
  • Sneak: Stealth skill rolls to move quietly while carrying the weapon.
  • Tight spaces: Penalty to attack rolls in a tight corridor (i.e. only 1 sq / 5' wide) or low ceilings (1 sq / 5').
  • Hands: Indicates whether the weapon is typically wielded with one or two hands.
