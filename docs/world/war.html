War In Rokugan

Strategies & Tactics

Rokugani armies are primarily infantry forces. The native Rokugani pony is not hardy enough to support full-scale cavalry warfare, although it can be used effectively for scouts or mounted infantry.

Thus, the only samurai who employ true cavalry tactics in Rokugan are the Unicorn, who brought back full-size horses from their long journey through the Burning Sands. Other Rokugani Armies developed some degree of anti-cavalry training and tactics in the centuries after the Unicorn returned to the Empire, but their lack of full-size horses prevented them from deploying any large-scale cavalry force of their own.

Armies

In practice, armies are more likely to be administrative and organizational in nature than to actually go into the field as a single unified force. Such large-scale deployments generally only take place during the Empire's rare periods of unrestricted warfare such as the Clan Wars.

When armies go into battle, regardless of their strenght, it is the smaller units - legions and companies - which form the primary units of tactical maneuver. They usually deploy in rectangular blocks, wider than they are deep. However, the Rokugani generally do not have the concepts like phalanx fighting or maintaing a "shield wall". It is expected that once two enemy units collide on the battlefield, the soldiers on each side will engage each other in personal combat. Consequently, the Rokugani march and advance in a more dispersed and open formation than Western medieval armies, and once contact is made, with the enemy, any formation will quickly break down into a sprawl of hundreds of small melees. Thus, battlefield tactics tend to focus more on pre-contact maneuvering, bringing more troops to bear on the decivise point through effective scouting and skillful march and deployment, and wearing downthe enemy with archery and magical attack prior to engagement, as well as on successfully withdrawing and rallying units after combat.
